📖 Activity 4: Read About Tom

Read the text about Tom, then answer the questions!

Meet Tom

Hello! My name is Tom. I am twelve years old and I am from Canada. I have brown hair and blue eyes. I am tall for my age. I have one sister. She is ten years old and her name is Emma.

Today the weather is sunny and warm. I like sunny days because I can play outside. When it's rainy, I stay inside and read books. I have many books in my room.

My family is very nice. My father is a teacher and my mother is a doctor. We have a dog named Max. He is very friendly and loves to play.

1. How old is Tom?

a) Ten years old
b) Twelve years old
c) Eleven years old

2. What color are Tom's eyes?

a) Brown
b) Blue
c) Green

3. What does Tom like to do on sunny days?

a) Play outside
b) Read books
c) Stay inside

🗣️ Activity 5: Answer Questions About Tom

Practice answering these questions about Tom in your own words. You can say your answers out loud or write them down.

Practice Questions:

  • Question 1: How old is Tom?

  • Question 3: What is Tom's sister's name?

💡 Remember:

• Answer in your own words
• Use complete sentences
• Don't copy directly from the text
• Practice saying your answers out loud!
